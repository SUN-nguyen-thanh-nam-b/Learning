# Third-party components

## TiMini-Print

`TiMini-Print-Command-Line-Windows-x86_64.exe` is redistributed unmodified.

- Source: https://github.com/Dejniel/TiMini-Print
- Licence: Apache License 2.0
- Role: speaks the PD-01's proprietary v5g BLE protocol

## TikTokLive

- Source: https://github.com/isaackogan/TikTokLive
- Role: unofficial TikTok LIVE event stream (follow events)

TikTokLive is an unofficial, reverse-engineered client. It is not affiliated
with or endorsed by TikTok, and it can break whenever TikTok changes its
webcast protocol.
